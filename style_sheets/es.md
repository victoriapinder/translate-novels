# Spanish — romántica contemporánea, neutral Latin American (Mexico first, readable in Spain)

- Address: "tú" for couples, friends, family, teammates, children; "usted" for strangers, bosses, elders, and formal
  business moments in the source (Mr./Ms. + surname); switch usted → tú at the first intimate/friendly beat and never
  revert. NEVER "vosotros/vosotras" or "os"; use "ustedes" for plural you everywhere.
- Honorifics: Mr. → Sr., Mrs./Ms. → Sra., Dr. → Dr./Dra. ("doctor/doctora" in dialogue).
- Quotation marks: dialogue with raya (—Habla —dijo él.) as Spanish-language fiction does; inner quotes «…» or “…”
  consistently (use “…”). Inverted marks ¿…? ¡…! always.
- Pet names: "babe/baby" → "nena" / "cariño" / "amor"; "sweetheart" → "cariño"/"mi amor"; "honey" → "cariño".
  Avoid Spain-only slang (tío, guay, vale) and Mexico-only slang that Spain won't read (güey, chido); neutral register.
- Register: natural, modern, spoken; no Anglicisms where a Spanish word is normal; avoid the passive.
- Sports: NFL/MLB/NHL terms stay in English where Spanish-language media does (quarterback, home run, touchdown,
  playoffs); "the team" → "el equipo".
- Measures → metric. Money stays in dollars.
- Chapter headings: "Chapter 3" → "Capítulo 3"; "Epilogue" → "Epílogo"; "Prologue" → "Prólogo".
