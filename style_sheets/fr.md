# French — romance contemporaine (France, lisible en Belgique/Suisse/Québec)

- Address: "tu" for couples, friends, family, teammates, children; "vous" for strangers, bosses, elders, formal
  business in the source; switch vous → tu at the first intimate/friendly beat (often after the first kiss or when a
  first name is offered) and never revert. Plural you → vous.
- Honorifics: Mr. → M. (monsieur in dialogue), Mrs./Ms. → Mme (madame), Dr. → Dr (docteur in dialogue).
- Quotation marks: French guillemets « … » with non-breaking spaces inside; dialogue may use tirets (— …) as French
  romance does — use « » for the first line and — for the exchange, consistently. Non-breaking space before ; : ! ?
- Pet names: "babe/baby" → "bébé" (common in French romance) / "ma belle" / "chérie"; "sweetheart" → "mon cœur" /
  "chérie"; "honey" → "chérie". Foreign pet names stay.
- Register: natural spoken French; avoid the passé simple in dialogue (passé composé); narration may use passé
  simple/imparfait as French romance does. Avoid Anglicisms where French words are normal; "un date" acceptable in
  casual dialogue.
- Sports: NFL/MLB/NHL terms stay in English (quarterback, home run, touchdown, playoffs); "the team" → "l'équipe".
- Measures → metric. Money stays in dollars.
- Chapter headings: "Chapter 3" → "Chapitre 3"; "Epilogue" → "Épilogue"; "Prologue" → "Prologue".
