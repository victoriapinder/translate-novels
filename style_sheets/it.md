# Italian — romance contemporaneo

- Address: "tu" for couples, friends, family, teammates; "Lei" (capital L) for strangers, bosses, elders, formal
  business in the source; switch Lei → tu at the first intimate/friendly beat ("diamoci del tu") and never revert.
- Honorifics: Mr. → signor, Mrs./Ms. → signora, Dr. → dottor/dottoressa ("dottore" in dialogue).
- Quotation marks: dialogue in caporali «…» as Italian trade fiction does; inner quotes “…”; ellipsis "…" attached;
  em-dash for interruptions.
- Pet names: "babe/baby" → "piccola" / "amore" / "tesoro"; "sweetheart" → "tesoro"; "honey" → "amore". Foreign pet
  names stay. Never "cara" as a translation of "babe" unless the source is Italian-flavoured.
- Register: natural spoken Italian, modern; avoid the passive; avoid heavy literary tenses in dialogue (passato
  prossimo in dialogue, passato remoto/imperfetto in narration is acceptable and standard for romance).
- Sports: NFL/MLB/NHL terms stay in English (quarterback, home run, touchdown, playoff); "the team" → "la squadra".
- Measures → metric. Money stays in dollars.
- Chapter headings: "Chapter 3" → "Capitolo 3"; "Epilogue" → "Epilogo"; "Prologue" → "Prologo".
