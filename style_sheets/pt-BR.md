# Brazilian Portuguese — romance contemporâneo (Brasil)

- Address: "você" for everyone in dialogue, including couples and family; "o senhor / a senhora" only for elderly,
  bosses, or deliberately formal/distant moments in the source (Mr./Mrs. + surname). Never "tu" conjugations.
- Honorifics: Mr. → Sr., Mrs./Ms. → Sra., Dr. → Dr./Dra. (doctors are addressed as "doutor/doutora" in dialogue).
- Quotation marks: dialogue with travessão (— Fala. — disse ele.) as Brazilian trade fiction does; internal quotes “…”.
  Ellipsis "…" attached; em-dash for interruptions.
- Pet names: "babe/baby" → "amor" / "gata" (playful) / "linda"; "sweetheart" → "querida/querido" or "amor";
  "honey" → "amor". Keep foreign pet names ("cara") as-is.
- Register: natural spoken Brazilian Portuguese (não → "não", "a gente" acceptable in casual dialogue, "tá"/"pra" only
  in very casual lines). No European Portuguese forms (never "estou a fazer", never "rapariga").
- Heat vocabulary: sensual romance register (as in Brazilian bestsellers), never clinical.
- Sports: NFL/MLB/NHL terms mostly stay in English (quarterback, home run, touchdown, playoffs); "the team" → "o time".
- Measures → metric. Money stays in dollars. American names/brands stay.
- Numbers, dates and times in Brazilian forms (19h, sete e meia).
- Chapter headings: "Chapter 3" → "Capítulo 3"; "Epilogue" → "Epílogo"; "Prologue" → "Prólogo".
